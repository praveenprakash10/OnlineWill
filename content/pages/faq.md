## Is OnlineWill.in a law firm?

No. OnlineWill.in publishes educational articles and is building a guided will-creation product. Content here is not legal advice.

## Can I write my own will?

Many people can prepare a will with clear information and the right formalities for their situation. Complex estates may need a qualified lawyer.

## Where will the will product live?

When the product launches, it will be on this site. Articles will stay under **Articles**.
